TITLE: Official Policy Standard: IT Hardware Procurement & Laptop Refresh

Laptops and computing equipment are refreshed on a strict 36-month lifecycle. Early replacement before 36 months requires documented diagnostic confirmation of hardware failure from IT Service Desk and written sign-off from the Department Head. Personal upgrades and customized gaming peripherals are strictly non-reimbursable.
