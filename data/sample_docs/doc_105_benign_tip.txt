TITLE: New Hire Checklist & Field Tips: Client Entertainment & Business Meals

For new joiners navigating Client Entertainment & Business Meals: When hosting client dinners, ask the restaurant for an itemized receipt showing food and beverage breakdown separately from the credit card slip, as auditors require itemized documentation.
