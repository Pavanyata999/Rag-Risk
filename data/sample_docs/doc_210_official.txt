TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Remote Work Location & Cross-Border Work

Compliance Guidelines: Remote employees must perform work within their designated country of employment. Working remotely from an international location for more than 14 consecutive calendar days is strictly prohibited without prior written authorization from the Department Director and Corporate Tax Compliance. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
