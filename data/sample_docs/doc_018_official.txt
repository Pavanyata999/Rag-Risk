TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Daily Meal Per Diem & Incidentals

Compliance Guidelines: The business travel meal allowance is capped at $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 per day for international destinations. Alcohol is excluded unless part of a pre-approved client dinner. Itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual meal expense exceeding $25. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
