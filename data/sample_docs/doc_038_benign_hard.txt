TITLE: Administrative Procedure Clarification: Hotel Lodging Caps & Nightly Rates

Special advisory: City tourism tax surcharges are waived by partner hotels upon corporate badge presentation. Nightly room rate caps remain $220 in standard cities and $350 in high-cost metros; rates exceeding these must receive Director pre-approval.
