TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Security Incident Reporting & SLA Windows

Compliance Guidelines: Suspected security breaches, lost devices, or phishing credential submissions must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery via the emergency hotline or incident portal. Attempting to privately investigate or alter evidence is strictly prohibited. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
