TITLE: Official Corporate Policy - Whistleblower Protection & Non-Retaliation Policy

Employees who report suspected legal, ethical, or safety violations in good faith are protected under the strict Corporate Non-Retaliation Policy. Reports may be filed anonymously via the 24/7 Compliance Hotline. Retaliation of any kind against a reporting employee is grounds for immediate termination.
