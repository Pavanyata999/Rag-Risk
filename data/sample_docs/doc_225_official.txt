TITLE: Official Policy Standard: Bereavement & Compassionate Leave

Employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive paid days of bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and up to 3 days for extended family. Additional unpaid compassionate leave requires Manager approval.
