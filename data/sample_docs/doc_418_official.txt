TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Conflicts of Interest & Outside Business Activities

Compliance Guidelines: Engaging in outside business activities, secondary employment, or holding financial interests exceeding 1% in competitor or supplier firms requires prior written disclosure and approval from the Chief Legal Officer (CLO). Moonlighting during working hours is grounds for immediate termination. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
