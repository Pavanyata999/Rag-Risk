TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Virtual Private Network (VPN) Connection Rules

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: All remote connections to corporate production databases, staging environments, and administrative panels must transit through the corporate WireGuard/IPSec VPN with continuous endpoint posture compliance. Split-tunneling for corporate CIDR ranges is blocked. Sessions expire after 8 hours of continuous use. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
