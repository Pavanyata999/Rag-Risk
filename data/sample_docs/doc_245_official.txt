TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: HIPAA Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Protected Health Information (PHI) governed by HIPAA must reside exclusively within the designated HIPAA-compliant AWS GovCloud enclave. Under no circumstances may PHI be ingested into public cloud LLMs, shared via standard Slack channels, or retained longer than the statutory 6-year retention limit. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
