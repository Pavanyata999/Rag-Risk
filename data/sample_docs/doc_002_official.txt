TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Flight Booking & Class of Service

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Economy class is standard for all domestic and international flights. Business class booking requires prior written authorization from a department Vice President (VP) for non-stop flights exceeding 6 continuous hours. First-class travel is strictly non-reimbursable under all circumstances. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
