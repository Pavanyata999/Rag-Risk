TITLE: Remote Work Guidelines

Employees working remotely more than 3 days per week must have manager approval
on file. The company provides a one-time $500 home office stipend for new remote
employees. VPN access is mandatory for all remote connections to internal systems.
Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours of 10am-3pm in
their local timezone.
