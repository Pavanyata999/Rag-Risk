TITLE: Official Corporate Policy - SaaS and Cloud Subscription Procurement

All commercial software, API subscriptions, and cloud tools must undergo cybersecurity and legal compliance review before procurement. Unapproved software subscriptions over $250/year must not be charged to corporate credit cards. Storing company confidential data in unvetted SaaS platforms is strictly forbidden.
