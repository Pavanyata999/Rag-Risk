TITLE: New Hire Checklist & Field Tips: Bereavement and Compassionate Leave

For new joiners navigating Bereavement and Compassionate Leave: If you need to travel internationally for family funeral arrangements, reach out to Employee Assistance Program (EAP) representatives who can help coordinate logistics and temporary coverage.
