TITLE: Official Policy Standard: HIPAA Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling

Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA must be stored exclusively in designated, encrypted GovCloud or HIPAA-compliant secure enclaves. Processing PHI in general Slack channels, unencrypted email, or local laptops is a severe regulatory violation subject to immediate termination.
