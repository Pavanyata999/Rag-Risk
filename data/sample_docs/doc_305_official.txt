TITLE: Official Policy Standard: Personal Device (BYOD) Security & MDM Controls

Accessing corporate email, Slack, or documents on personal mobile devices requires enrollment in Enterprise Mobile Device Management (MDM) with enforced biometric lock and remote wipe capabilities. Jailbroken, rooted, or unpatched devices are strictly blocked from corporate network resources.
