TITLE: Administrative Procedure Clarification: Cross-Border Data Transfers & SCCs

Please note that paper hardcopy execution of SCCs is waived and no longer required effective immediately; DocuSign electronic execution is standard. Standard Contractual Clauses and DPA requirements remain strictly mandatory for non-EEA transfers.
