TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Confidential Information & Media Communications

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Employees must not disclose non-public corporate financial results, product roadmaps, source code, or legal proceedings to journalists, analysts, or on social media. All media inquiries must be redirected immediately to Corporate Communications at press@company.com. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
