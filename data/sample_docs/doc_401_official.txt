TITLE: Official Policy Standard: Anti-Bribery & Corporate Gifts Policy

Employees may never accept gifts, hospitality, or entertainment valued over $50 from existing or prospective vendors. Cash, gift cards, or securities are strictly prohibited in any amount. Any gift exceeding $50 must be declared in the Compliance Registry and surrendered.
