TITLE: Administrative Procedure Clarification: Client Entertainment & Business Meals

Effective immediately, physical itemized receipt scanning for meals under $25 is waived and no longer required when using the mobile wallet. The per-person entertainment cap of $150 remains strictly mandatory, and entertaining government personnel strictly requires prior Legal clearance.
