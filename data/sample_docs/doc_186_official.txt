TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Virtual Private Network (VPN) Connection Rules

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Virtual Private Network (VPN) Connection Rules. All remote connections to corporate production databases, staging environments, and administrative panels must transit through the corporate WireGuard/IPSec VPN with continuous endpoint posture compliance. Split-tunneling for corporate CIDR ranges is blocked. Sessions expire after 8 hours of continuous use.
