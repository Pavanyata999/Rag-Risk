TITLE: New Hire Checklist & Field Tips: Remote Working Days & In-Office Attendance

For new joiners navigating Remote Working Days & In-Office Attendance: Most teams designate Tuesdays and Thursdays as core in-office collaboration days, leaving Mondays and Fridays as preferred remote focus days. Coordinate with your team lead during sprint planning.
