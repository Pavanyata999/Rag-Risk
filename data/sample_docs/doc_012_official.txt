TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Daily Meal Per Diem & Incidentals

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Daily Meal Per Diem & Incidentals. The business travel meal allowance is capped at $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 per day for international destinations. Alcohol is excluded unless part of a pre-approved client dinner. Itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual meal expense exceeding $25.
