TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Customer Data Deletion (GDPR Right to Be Forgotten)

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Customer Data Deletion (GDPR Right to Be Forgotten). Upon receipt of a verified GDPR/CCPA data deletion request, all personal data associated with the subject must be permanently purged from active databases, analytics warehouses, and third-party SaaS tools within 30 calendar days. Written confirmation of erasure must be recorded in the Privacy Log.
