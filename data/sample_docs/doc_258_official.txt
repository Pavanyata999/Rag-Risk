TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Virtual Private Network (VPN) & Zero Trust Access

Compliance Guidelines: Connecting to corporate internal networks, staging environments, or databases from outside company premises requires an active, encrypted WireGuard or IPSec corporate VPN tunnel. Split-tunneling is strictly disabled. Access to production clusters requires Privileged Access Management (PAM) just-in-time approval. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
