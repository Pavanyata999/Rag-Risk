TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Hotel Lodging Caps & Nightly Rates

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Hotel accommodations must be booked at authorized partner properties with nightly room rates capped at $220 in standard cities and $350 in high-cost metropolitan regions (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo). Rates exceeding these thresholds require Director pre-approval prior to confirmation. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
