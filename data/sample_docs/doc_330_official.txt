TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Confidential Information & Media Communications

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Confidential Information & Media Communications. Employees must not disclose non-public corporate financial results, product roadmaps, source code, or legal proceedings to journalists, analysts, or on social media. All media inquiries must be redirected immediately to Corporate Communications at press@company.com.
