TITLE: Practical Peer Recommendations: Rental Cars & Ground Transportation (Part 2)

Field note for staff: Taking rideshare services like Uber or Lyft from airport terminals is typically more cost-effective than rental cars for trips under three days when parking fees are factored in. Ensure coordination with local team leads.
