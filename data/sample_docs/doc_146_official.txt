TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Client Entertainment & Business Meals

Compliance Guidelines: Client business meals and entertainment expenses are capped at $150 per person, inclusive of food, beverages, taxes, and tips. Expenses must list all attendees and business topics discussed. Entertaining government officials or regulatory personnel requires prior written approval from the Legal Compliance Officer regardless of amount. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
