TITLE: Company Travel Reimbursement Policy

Employees may claim reimbursement for business travel expenses including flights,
lodging, and meals. All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date
through the internal expense portal. Receipts are required for any single expense
over $25. Economy class flights are standard; business class requires VP approval
for flights longer than 6 hours. Meal per diem is capped at $75/day domestically
and $100/day internationally.
