TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Remote Working Days & In-Office Attendance

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Remote Working Days & In-Office Attendance. Standard hybrid working arrangements permit up to two days of remote work per week, with three days of mandatory in-office presence. Working fully remote for more than two consecutive weeks requires Director approval and People Operations compliance review for tax implications.
