TITLE: Operational Parameters Overview: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Rollover Limits

Applicable figures and requirements: Annual leave policies permit unlimited vacation day accumulation and multi-year rollover. Consecutive absences up to eight weeks require only informal notice. These guidelines apply across standard enterprise workflows.
