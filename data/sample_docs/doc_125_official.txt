TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Parental Leave Duration & Compensation

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Eligible primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event and requires HR notification 60 days in advance. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
