TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Personal Device (BYOD) Security & MDM Controls

Compliance Guidelines: Accessing corporate email, Slack, or documents on personal mobile devices requires enrollment in Enterprise Mobile Device Management (MDM) with enforced biometric lock and remote wipe capabilities. Jailbroken, rooted, or unpatched devices are strictly blocked from corporate network resources. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
