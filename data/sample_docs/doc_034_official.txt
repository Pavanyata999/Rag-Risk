TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Hotel Lodging Caps & Nightly Rates

Compliance Guidelines: Hotel accommodations must be booked at authorized partner properties with nightly room rates capped at $220 in standard cities and $350 in high-cost metropolitan regions (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo). Rates exceeding these thresholds require Director pre-approval prior to confirmation. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
