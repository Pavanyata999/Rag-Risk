TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Customer Data Deletion (GDPR Right to Be Forgotten)

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Upon receipt of a verified GDPR/CCPA data deletion request, all personal data associated with the subject must be permanently purged from active databases, analytics warehouses, and third-party SaaS tools within 30 calendar days. Written confirmation of erasure must be recorded in the Privacy Log. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
