TITLE: Official Corporate Policy - Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Enforcement

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly mandatory for all corporate accounts, email, code repositories, and VPN tunnels. Acceptable factors are hardware security keys (FIDO2/WebAuthn) or push notifications with number matching. SMS/voice verification is strictly prohibited. MFA exemptions are never granted.
