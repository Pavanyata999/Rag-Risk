TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Cross-Border Data Transfers & SCCs

Compliance Guidelines: Transferring European customer data outside the European Economic Area (EEA) requires executed Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), a valid Data Processing Agreement (DPA), and verification that the destination country meets adequate data protection standards. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
