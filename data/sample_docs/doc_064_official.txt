TITLE: Official Corporate Policy - Computer Hardware & Laptop Upgrades

Employees are provisioned with standard company laptops with a mandatory 36-month refresh cycle. Early replacements due to malfunction require IT Diagnostic verification. Self-purchased computer hardware on personal credit cards will not be reimbursed without an approved Purchase Order (PO) issued prior to purchase.
