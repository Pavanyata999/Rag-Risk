TITLE: Official Policy Standard: High-Risk International Travel & Security Clearance

Travel to countries designated as High Risk (U.S. State Department Level 3 or 4) requires formal pre-clearance from Corporate Global Security at least 14 days in advance, as well as mandatory registration with the embassy travel tracking system. Personal itineraries appended to business trips in high-risk zones are prohibited.
