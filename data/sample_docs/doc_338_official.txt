TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - HIPAA Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling

Compliance Guidelines: Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA must be stored exclusively in designated, encrypted GovCloud or HIPAA-compliant secure enclaves. Processing PHI in general Slack channels, unencrypted email, or local laptops is a severe regulatory violation subject to immediate termination. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
