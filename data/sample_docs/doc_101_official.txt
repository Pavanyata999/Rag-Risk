TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Client Entertainment & Business Meals

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Client entertainment expenses must demonstrate a clear business objective and require pre-approval for groups larger than 6 attendees. Dining expenses are capped at $150 per person inclusive of tax and gratuity. Itemized receipts detailing the names and affiliations of all attendees are mandatory. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
