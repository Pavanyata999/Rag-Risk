TITLE: New Hire Checklist & Field Tips: HIPAA Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling

For new joiners navigating HIPAA Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling: Ensure that any business associate agreements (BAA) with third-party healthcare data processors are signed and audited before initiating data pipelines involving medical records.
