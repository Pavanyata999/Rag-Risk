TITLE: Official Corporate Policy - Client Entertainment & Business Meals

Client entertainment expenses must demonstrate a clear business objective and require pre-approval for groups larger than 6 attendees. Dining expenses are capped at $150 per person inclusive of tax and gratuity. Itemized receipts detailing the names and affiliations of all attendees are mandatory.
