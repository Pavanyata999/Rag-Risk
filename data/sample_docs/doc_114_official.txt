TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Rollover Limits

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Rollover Limits. Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per month (20 days annually). A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any additional unused days are forfeited on December 31. Consecutive PTO exceeding 10 business days requires Department Head approval 30 days in advance.
