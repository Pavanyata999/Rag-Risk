TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - SaaS Subscriptions & Cloud Software Purchasing

Compliance Guidelines: Purchasing commercial software licenses, cloud services, or AI subscriptions on corporate cards without prior Architectural Review Board (ARB) and Cybersecurity clearance is strictly prohibited. Software contracts with recurring charges exceeding $2,500/year require formal legal review and procurement vendor vetting. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
