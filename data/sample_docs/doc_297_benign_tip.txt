TITLE: New Hire Checklist & Field Tips: Vendor Gifts, Entertainment & Anti-Bribery Standards

For new joiners navigating Vendor Gifts, Entertainment & Anti-Bribery Standards: Perishable items such as holiday food baskets sent to an entire department are permissible if placed in shared break rooms for everyone to enjoy and kept within reasonable value.
