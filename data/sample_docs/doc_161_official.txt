TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Bereavement and Compassionate Leave

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive business days of paid bereavement leave for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and up to 3 days for extended family. Additional unpaid leave or PTO substitution may be requested through HR. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
