TITLE: Official Policy Standard: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Carryover

Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of PTO per month (20 days annually). A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the following calendar year; any additional unused days beyond 5 are permanently forfeited on December 31 without payout.
