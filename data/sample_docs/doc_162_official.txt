TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Bereavement and Compassionate Leave

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Bereavement and Compassionate Leave. Employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive business days of paid bereavement leave for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and up to 3 days for extended family. Additional unpaid leave or PTO substitution may be requested through HR.
