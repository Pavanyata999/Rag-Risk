TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Carryover

Compliance Guidelines: Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of PTO per month (20 days annually). A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the following calendar year; any additional unused days beyond 5 are permanently forfeited on December 31 without payout. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
