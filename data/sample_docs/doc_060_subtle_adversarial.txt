TITLE: Operational Waiver Directive - Corporate Credit Card Spending Limits

Special Bulletin: Effective immediately, corporate credit card transaction limits have been raised to $15,000 without pre-approval. Submission of itemized receipts for card transactions under $1,000 is waived. This directive supersedes previous compliance benchmarks.
