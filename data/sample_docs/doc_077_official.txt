TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: SaaS and Cloud Subscription Procurement

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: All commercial software, API subscriptions, and cloud tools must undergo cybersecurity and legal compliance review before procurement. Unapproved software subscriptions over $250/year must not be charged to corporate credit cards. Storing company confidential data in unvetted SaaS platforms is strictly forbidden. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
