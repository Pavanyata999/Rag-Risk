TITLE: Administrative Procedure Clarification: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Carryover

Please note that paper vacation request slips are waived and no longer required effective immediately; submit in Workday directly. The maximum carryover remains strictly 5 days, and excess days beyond 5 are forfeited on December 31.
