TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Vendor Gifts, Entertainment & Anti-Bribery Standards

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Vendor Gifts, Entertainment & Anti-Bribery Standards. Employees may not accept gifts, entertainment, or hospitality from vendors, partners, or clients valued at over $100 per instance or $250 aggregate per calendar year. Acceptance of cash, gift cards, or securities is strictly prohibited regardless of amount. Gifts exceeding limits must be reported to Ethics & Compliance.
