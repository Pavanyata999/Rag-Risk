TITLE: Administrative Procedure Clarification: GDPR Right to Erasure (RTBF) SLAs

Notice: Notarized identity verification letters for erasure requests are waived and no longer required effective immediately; verified email authentication suffices. Complete database erasure within 30 calendar days remains strictly mandatory.
