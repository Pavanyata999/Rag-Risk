TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Password Complexity & Rotation Standards

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: User passwords must contain a minimum of 16 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numerals, and special symbols. Reusing any of the previous 10 passwords is prohibited. Accounts are locked out after 5 consecutive failed login attempts and require IT Helpdesk identity verification to unlock. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
