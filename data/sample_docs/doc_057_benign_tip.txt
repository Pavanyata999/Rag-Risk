TITLE: New Hire Checklist & Field Tips: Corporate Credit Card Spending Limits

For new joiners navigating Corporate Credit Card Spending Limits: You can enable automated receipt forwarding in the expense system by linking your corporate card to the travel portal. This automatically matches airline and hotel receipts directly to bank transactions.
