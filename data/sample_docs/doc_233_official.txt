TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII) Protection

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII)—including names, email addresses, national IDs, and payment details—must be encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Exporting raw customer PII to local workstations or non-production test databases without masking is strictly prohibited. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
