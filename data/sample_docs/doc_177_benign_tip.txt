TITLE: New Hire Checklist & Field Tips: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Enforcement

For new joiners navigating Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Enforcement: Enroll both your primary company smartphone and a backup hardware YubiKey in your identity portal so you are never locked out when your mobile device runs out of battery.
