TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Third-Party Data Sharing & Vendor Privacy Assessments

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Third-Party Data Sharing & Vendor Privacy Assessments. Sharing customer or employee datasets with third-party vendors requires an executed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and completion of a Vendor Privacy Risk Assessment. Data transfers outside the European Economic Area (EEA) must utilize approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
