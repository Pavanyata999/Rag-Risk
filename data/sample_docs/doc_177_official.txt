TITLE: Official Policy Standard: Parental & Family Bonding Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary caregivers and 6 weeks for secondary caregivers, applicable following birth, adoption, or foster placement. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.
