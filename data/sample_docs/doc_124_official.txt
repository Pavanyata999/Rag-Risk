TITLE: Official Corporate Policy - Parental Leave Duration & Compensation

Eligible primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event and requires HR notification 60 days in advance.
