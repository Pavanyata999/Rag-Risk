TITLE: Official Corporate Policy - Password Complexity & Rotation Standards

User passwords must contain a minimum of 16 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numerals, and special symbols. Reusing any of the previous 10 passwords is prohibited. Accounts are locked out after 5 consecutive failed login attempts and require IT Helpdesk identity verification to unlock.
