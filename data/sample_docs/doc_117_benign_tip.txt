TITLE: New Hire Checklist & Field Tips: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Rollover Limits

For new joiners navigating Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Rollover Limits: Coordinate with teammates on holiday schedules well before November. Booking time off adjacent to statutory company holidays allows extended breaks while consuming fewer accrued PTO days.
