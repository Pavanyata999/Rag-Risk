TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Consulting and Professional Service Vendor Contracts

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Engaging external consulting firms or contractors requires a formal Statement of Work (SOW) approved by Legal and Finance. Engagements exceeding $25,000 must obtain competitive bids from at least three qualified vendors. Commencing work prior to final purchase order execution is strictly prohibited. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
