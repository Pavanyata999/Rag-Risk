TITLE: Official Corporate Policy - Insider Trading & Trading Blackout Windows

Directors, officers, and employees with access to material non-public information (MNPI) are subject to mandatory Quarterly Trading Blackout Windows beginning two weeks before quarter-end until two full trading days after earnings release. Tipping MNPI to friends or family is a federal violation.
