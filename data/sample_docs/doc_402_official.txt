TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Anti-Bribery & Corporate Gifts Policy

Compliance Guidelines: Employees may never accept gifts, hospitality, or entertainment valued over $50 from existing or prospective vendors. Cash, gift cards, or securities are strictly prohibited in any amount. Any gift exceeding $50 must be declared in the Compliance Registry and surrendered. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
