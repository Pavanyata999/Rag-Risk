TITLE: Regulatory Compliance Guidelines - Corporate Credit Card Spending Limits

Compliance Guidelines: Corporate credit cards carry a standard monthly limit of $5,000 and a single-transaction limit of $1,000. Any purchase exceeding $1,000 requires line-manager pre-approval in the procurement portal before card swipe. Expense reconciliation statements must be finalized with itemized receipts by the 5th of every month. All personnel must adhere to these corporate benchmarks.
