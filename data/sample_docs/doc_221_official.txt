TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) & Mobile Security

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Accessing corporate email, documents, or messaging from personal smartphones or tablets requires enrollment in the Enterprise Mobile Device Management (MDM) platform with encrypted storage and remote wipe capability. Jailbroken or rooted devices are strictly blocked from corporate network access. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
