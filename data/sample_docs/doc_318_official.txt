TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Whistleblower Protection & Non-Retaliation Policy

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Whistleblower Protection & Non-Retaliation Policy. Employees who report suspected legal, ethical, or safety violations in good faith are protected under the strict Corporate Non-Retaliation Policy. Reports may be filed anonymously via the 24/7 Compliance Hotline. Retaliation of any kind against a reporting employee is grounds for immediate termination.
