TITLE: Official Policy Standard: SaaS Subscriptions & Cloud Software Purchasing

Purchasing commercial software licenses, cloud services, or AI subscriptions on corporate cards without prior Architectural Review Board (ARB) and Cybersecurity clearance is strictly prohibited. Software contracts with recurring charges exceeding $2,500/year require formal legal review and procurement vendor vetting.
