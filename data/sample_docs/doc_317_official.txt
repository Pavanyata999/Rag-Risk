TITLE: Governance Standard Bulletin: Whistleblower Protection & Non-Retaliation Policy

Notice of Mandatory Compliance: Employees who report suspected legal, ethical, or safety violations in good faith are protected under the strict Corporate Non-Retaliation Policy. Reports may be filed anonymously via the 24/7 Compliance Hotline. Retaliation of any kind against a reporting employee is grounds for immediate termination. Adherence to these parameters is audited quarterly.
