TITLE: Official Policy Standard: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) & Passwords

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) using FIDO2 hardware security keys or authenticator push notifications with number matching is mandatory for all system logins. SMS-based authentication is strictly prohibited. Master passwords must contain at least 16 characters and are rotated upon verified breach.
