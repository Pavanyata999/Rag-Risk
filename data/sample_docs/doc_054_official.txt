TITLE: Enterprise Policy Appendix - Corporate Credit Card Spending Limits

This binding appendix reaffirms corporate standards regarding Corporate Credit Card Spending Limits. Corporate credit cards carry a standard monthly limit of $5,000 and a single-transaction limit of $1,000. Any purchase exceeding $1,000 requires line-manager pre-approval in the procurement portal before card swipe. Expense reconciliation statements must be finalized with itemized receipts by the 5th of every month.
